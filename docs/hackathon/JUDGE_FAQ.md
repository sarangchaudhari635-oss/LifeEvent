## 1. Technical & AI Architecture Questions

### Q1: "How do you guarantee that your AI won't hallucinate coverage numbers or give bad financial advice to a desperate family?"
**Answer:**  
We enforce a **strict architectural quarantine between natural language reasoning and financial arithmetic**:
1. **Zero Arithmetic Authority:** The LLM is technically forbidden from doing math. Prompts forbid producing rupee numbers. The model only extracts variables (`allowed_room_rent`, `stay_days`, `copay_percent`).
2. **Deterministic Python Calculator:** 100% of the money math (IRDAI room-rent proportional deduction, 10% non-payable consumables, co-pay) is computed deterministically in Python (`backend/app/core/gap_calculator.py`).
3. **The Citation Gate:** Every coverage claim generated by Gemini must cite a verbatim sentence, document name, page number, and clause ref. Our backend runs a character-level normalized substring search against the source PDF page. If the sentence does not match verbatim, the claim is marked `Unverified` and its payout is forced to ₹0.00.

### Q2: "Isn't this just a prompt wrapper around Google Gemini 2.0?"
**Answer:**  
Not at all. A prompt wrapper takes a PDF and asks a model to answer questions in chat. 

LifeEvent AI is an **event-driven operating system**:
- It compiles user context into an executable **Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG)** of tasks via LangGraph StateGraph.
- It dynamically re-plans workflow dependencies when out-of-order events (e.g., TPA queries) arrive.
- It runs mathematical **Verhoeff $D_5$ permutation algorithms** to detect genuine Aadhaar numbers and mask them before network egress.
- It enforces a **Customer Approval Gateway** requiring cryptographic parameter hashes and step-up authentication for consequential financial actions.
- It persists state in **Supabase PostgreSQL** with `pgvector` HNSW indexes and Row Level Security (RLS).

---

### Q3: "What happens if the Gemini API experiences network latency, rate-limiting, or an outage during a hospital emergency?"
**Answer:**  
We architected a **Dual-Engine Router with an Offline Deterministic Fixture Shield**. If the Google GenAI API times out or returns an error, the router automatically falls back to pre-compiled, verified scenario fixtures without throwing a 500 error or stalling the user interface. The user is guaranteed 100% platform availability.

## 2. Regulatory, Privacy & Security Questions

### Q4: "How does LifeEvent AI comply with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023?"
**Answer:**  
Compliance is built into the architecture:
- **Notice & Consent (Section 5 & 6):** Multilingual notices in English, Hindi, and Marathi generating digital consent artifacts stored in immutable audit logs.
- **Data Minimization (Section 8):** Raw photos of citizen identity cards (Aadhaar/PAN) are processed transiently in volatile memory to extract the masked sequence and are permanently discarded from disk and storage.
- **Right to Erasure (Section 12):** We implement an active, one-click hard delete endpoint (`DELETE /api/journey/{id}`) that cascades through PostgreSQL tables and Supabase Storage private buckets, permanently purging all records.

---

### Q5: "Why did you use the Verhoeff checksum algorithm instead of a standard 12-digit regex?"
**Answer:**  
A naive regex like `\b\d{12}\b` creates high false positives: it redacts 12-digit health insurance policy numbers, bank transaction reference numbers (UTR/RRN), and phone numbers formatted with international prefixes. 

The **Verhoeff algorithm** utilizes the mathematical dihedral permutation group $D_5$ to validate the 12th check digit. Only genuine Indian Aadhaar numbers pass the checksum and are masked to `XXXX-XXXX-1234`. Non-Aadhaar numbers pass through untouched.

### Q6: "How do you comply with the RBI Digital Lending Guidelines (DLG) for the emergency credit flow?"
**Answer:**  
Under RBI regulations:
1. LifeEvent AI operates strictly as a **Lending Service Provider (LSP)** interface. We do not carry balance-sheet risk or lend our own capital.
2. All loans are underwritten and disbursed directly by partner **Regulated Entities (REs)** (Banks or NBFCs).
3. Funds are disbursed directly to the hospital's verified billing escrow account—funds never pass through LifeEvent AI accounts.
4. We display the standardized **Key Fact Statement (KFS)** detailing all-inclusive APR and cooling-off periods before the customer authorizes the transaction.

## 3. Business Model & Integration Questions

### Q7: "How will you connect to insurance companies and TPAs that lack open APIs?"
**Answer:**  
We deploy a **Provider Abstraction Layer** with three tiers:
1. **Tier 1 (Simulated Drivers):** Active now for hackathon demonstration.
2. **Tier 2 (National Health Claims Exchange - NHCX):** The Government of India and NHA are standardizing claims exchange via NHCX using HL7 FHIR APIs. We align with NHCX profiles (`Claim`, `ClaimResponse`, `Coverage`).
3. **Tier 3 (Counselor Assisted Workflow):** In hospitals where TPAs operate via email or web portals, our Counselor B2B Console serves as the digital bridge, allowing the hospital TPA desk to log queries and dispatch documents directly.

---

### Q8: "What is your business model? Who pays for LifeEvent AI?"
**Answer:**  
We operate a **B2B2C dual-revenue model**:
1. **B2B Hospital SaaS Subscription (Primary):** Multi-specialty hospitals pay **₹25,000/month per hospital branch** for the Counselor B2B Console. Hospitals eagerly pay because it reduces emergency counter clearance time from 95 minutes to under 15 minutes, eliminates discharge billing disputes, and accelerates bed turnover.
2. **Fintech Origination Fees (Secondary):** A sub-1% origination fee paid by partner NBFCs upon successful disbursement of emergency bridge credit lines.
- **For Patients and Families:** The service is **100% free**, ensuring zero barrier to entry in medical emergencies.
